# Attribution

Every non-code asset in TuneCraft is listed here with its author, source and licence.

**Policy:** TuneCraft contains no Songbird code, artwork, icons, logos or text, and no
artwork, logos or trademarks of the ArtCraft apps. Its icon, its in-app mark and every icon
in the interface are original, drawn in code (`src/theme.rs`) or by
`packaging/make_icon.py`.

## Rust dependencies

294 crates (Apple Silicon build, `packaging/licences.py`). All are under permissive
licences: mostly MIT and/or Apache-2.0, plus Zlib, ISC, BSD, Unlicense, Unicode-3.0 and
CDLA-Permissive-2.0. File-level copyleft only:

| Crate | Licence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `symphonia` and its codec/format crates 0.5 | MPL-2.0 | audio decoding (through `rodio`), used unmodified |
| `option-ext` 0.2 | MPL-2.0 | through `dirs`, used unmodified |
| `self_cell` 1.3 | Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only | used under Apache-2.0 |

Main direct dependencies: `eframe`/`egui` (UI), `wry` (system WebKit view), `rodio` (audio
output), `lofty` (tags), `image`, `ureq` (downloads), `rfd` (file dialogs), `arboard`
(clipboard), `serde`/`serde_json`, `walkdir`, `dirs`, and on macOS `objc2`, `objc2-web-kit`,
`objc2-app-kit`, `objc2-foundation`, `block2` (page snapshots).
